## Core services

### Mechanical engineering

We design robust HVAC&R, plumbing, fire-protection, boiler-plant, and lift systems tailored for complex modern buildings.

Detailed services:

- Heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration (HVAC&R)
- Hot and cold water plumbing, sanitary drainage, and greywater recycling
- Fire-protection systems including sprinklers, standpipes, fire pumps, and gaseous suppression
- Reverse-osmosis and boiler plants
- Fuel-service-station systems and LP-gas systems
- Passenger and goods lifts, escalators, and elevator systems

### Electrical engineering

We design safe, efficient electrical and ELV systems for modern facilities, with a focus on resilience, continuity, safety, and comfort.

Detailed services:

- Low-voltage power distribution
- Standby generators, automatic transfer switches, and UPS systems
- Interior and exterior LED lighting and emergency egress lighting
- Earthing, bonding, surge, and lightning protection
- Fire-alarm and detection systems
- Structured cabling, CCTV, access control, and integrated ELV security systems

### Civil engineering

Our civil team delivers water, transport, drainage, treatment, and infrastructure systems for reliable and sustainable communities.

Detailed services:

- Water reticulation, dams, and irrigation design
- Rural and urban infrastructure development
- Water and sewer treatment plants
- Pump stations and pumping mains
- Geotechnical and drainage design
- Environmental engineering
- Groundwater condition assessments, hydrogeological surveys, and testing
- Road design and transport corridors

### Structural engineering

We engineer resilient structures for utilities, industry, buildings, storage, and transport infrastructure.

Detailed services:

- Steel structures including transmission grids, cellular towers, and mast frames
- Storage structures including silos and tanks
- Liquid-retaining structures, reservoirs, and basins
- Road and rail bridges
- Site investigations and structural assessments
- Single- and multi-storey residential, industrial, and commercial buildings

### Project management

From initiation to handover, we coordinate the decisions, controls, and site activities that keep projects moving.

Detailed services:

- Project initiation and feasibility studies
- Scope, time, cost, quality, and risk management
- Procurement strategy and tender documentation
- Contractor adjudication and contract administration
- Site supervision and progress monitoring
- Commissioning, snagging, and handover coordination

### BIM technology

We leverage Building Information Modeling tools up to LOD 600 to improve precision, coordination, code compliance, and project delivery.
